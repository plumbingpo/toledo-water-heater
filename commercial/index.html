Hot Water for Kitchens, Restaurants, and Food Facilities

In a restaurant, the dish machine, the sinks, and the handwashing stations all pull from the same supply, and a heater that's undersized or failing reveals itself as a dish machine that can't hit temperature and stacks of dirty plates. A high-temp dish machine usually needs final rinse water near 180°F, which usually means a booster heater working alongside the main supply. We handle high-input tanks, tankless racks, booster heaters, and peak-demand sizing, and we schedule every visit around your prep and service times.

A dish room without enough hot water can't sanitize, and health inspectors check the handwashing sinks right away. Our service keeps temperatures and recovery where your kitchen needs them, backed by service logs and written reports after each visit. Those records show a well-maintained kitchen to inspectors and landlords.

Replacements and maintenance visits are planned alongside owners, managers, and kitchen leads, so everything fits your operating schedule. When a job can't happen during service, we install overnight or before opening, so the kitchen opens the next day with hot water ready.

Safety

Safe Installations, Documented Service

Commercial property owners have a duty of care to provide safe conditions for employees, customers, and tenants. A poorly installed or neglected water heater can result in carbon monoxide from a blocked or undersized vent, scalding at the tap, bacterial growth in tanks kept too cool, and costly water damage from a failed tank. Toledo Water Heater provides commercial installations and inspections that cover venting and combustion air, gas piping sizing, relief valve and discharge line routing, expansion tanks, thermostatic mixing valves, drain pans, seismic strapping where required, and permits and inspections. Our written reports identify risks, assign priority levels, and recommend specific preventive actions.

Consistent professional service protects the people and property you're responsible for, and acting on expert recommendations keeps minor issues from becoming emergencies. It also creates a record. We keep service logs, temperature records, and a documentation binder for every account, with more frequent visits for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, laundries, and mechanical rooms. When a landlord, insurer, or inspector asks for proof of due diligence, it's ready.
